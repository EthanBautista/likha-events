# Adding a Setup from your phone

This is how to put a new Setup on the website using just your phone. It takes about five minutes. You'll need your photos in your phone's photo gallery.

## 1. Open the editor

1. On your phone, go to **likhaevents.ca/admin**.
2. Tap **Login with GitHub** and sign in with your GitHub account. You only need to do this once on each phone.

## 2. Start a new Setup

1. Tap **Setups**, then tap **New Setup**.
2. **Caption**: write one short line about the Setup, such as "Sage and ivory baby shower".
3. **Event type**: pick Baby shower, Birthday or another type from the list.

## 3. Add the photos

1. Under **Cover photo**, tap the button to add a photo. This opens Cloudinary, where the website's photos are kept.
2. The first time, Cloudinary asks you to log in. Use the Likha Events Cloudinary login.
3. Tap **Upload**, then choose the photo from your phone. Pick your best photo for the cover: it's the one people see first in the gallery.
4. When it has uploaded, select it and tap **Insert**.
5. Under **More photos**, do the same for up to 8 more photos, such as the cake table and close-ups. You can choose several at once.

Don't worry about photo size. Big photos are made smaller automatically when they upload.

## 4. Finish and publish

1. **Feature on homepage**: turn this on if you'd like the Setup shown on the homepage.
2. **Event date**: add the date of the event if you know it. Setups are shown newest first.
3. Leave **Sample** turned off. It's only for the example Setups the site started with.
4. Tap **Save**.

The Setup appears on the website after a couple of minutes.

## If something goes wrong

- **It won't save**: look for a message in red next to a field. Every field needs filling in except More photos, Feature on homepage and Event date.
- **"Choose photos from Cloudinary"**: add the photo with the button in step 3, rather than pasting a link to a photo somewhere else.
- **To change or remove a Setup**: tap **Setups**, tap the Setup, then change it and tap **Save**, or delete it from the menu.
- **Still stuck**: send Ethan a screenshot of the screen.
