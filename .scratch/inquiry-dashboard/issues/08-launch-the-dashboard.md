# 08: Launch the Inquiry dashboard

**What to build:** The manual steps to put the Inquiry dashboard live and hand it to the owner. See the spec: `.scratch/inquiry-dashboard/spec.md` and ADR 0004.

**Blocked by:** 01–07, and the site's launch (`.scratch/likha-events-website/issues/16-launch.md`)

**Status:** ready-for-human

- [ ] Netlify Blobs and Functions are on for the site in the developer's Netlify account, and within free-tier limits
- [ ] Send a real Inquiry with photos: it appears on the dashboard as New with its photos, and the email still arrives
- [ ] The owner logs in to `/admin/inquiries` on his phone with his GitHub account, and the login is remembered
- [ ] A GitHub account without push access is refused
- [ ] The owner guide explains Inquiry statuses, adding an Inquiry from Instagram or Facebook, and the money fields
