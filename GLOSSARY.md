# Likha Events

Likha Events designs and sets up decorations and backdrops for customers' events (baby showers, birthdays and more) in the Greater Toronto Area. This site shows Likha Events' work and turns visitors into inquiries. The logo shows the short form "Likha"; everywhere else the business is "Likha Events".

## Language

**Setup**:
One decoration job Likha Events did for a customer's event, shown in the gallery with its photos.
_Avoid_: Project, event, work, décor

**Event type**:
The kind of occasion a Setup was made for, such as Baby shower, Birthday or Other.
_Avoid_: Category, occasion

**Package**:
A priced bundle of décor that Likha Events designs and sets up, starting at $450.
_Avoid_: Plan, bundle, tier

**Rental**:
A décor item a customer hires on its own, without Likha Events designing or setting it up.
_Avoid_: Hire, item, add-on

**Inquiry**:
A customer's request for a quote, sent through the site's contact form.
_Avoid_: Booking, lead, request

**Corporate client**:
A business, such as a mall, office or brand, that books Likha Events, as opposed to someone booking for a personal occasion. Not an Event type.
_Avoid_: Business customer, B2B

**Service area**:
The region Likha Events covers at no extra charge: the whole GTA (Toronto, Durham, York, Peel and Halton). Events outside it pay a travel fee.
_Avoid_: Coverage, delivery zone

**Down payment**:
The 50% non-refundable payment that holds a customer's date.
_Avoid_: Deposit, reservation fee
